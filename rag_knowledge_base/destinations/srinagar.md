# Srinagar, Jammu and Kashmir
**Category:** Nature  
**Best Season to Visit:** April - October  
**Average Daily Budget:** INR 3200 per person  
**Average Temperature:** 13.0 °C  

## Overview
Heaven on Earth featuring serene Dal Lake houseboats, Mughal gardens, and shikara rides.

## Key Tourist Attractions
- Sightseeing Highlights: Exploration of cultural monuments, viewpoints, and nature trails.
- Unique Landmarks: Signature heritage and scenic points celebrated across Jammu and Kashmir.
- Outdoor Activities: Trekking, boating, photography, and local market strolling.

## Cultural & Historical Context
Srinagar has deep regional heritage representing the cultural vibrancy of Jammu and Kashmir. Local residents celebrate regional festivals with music, handloom crafts, and authentic cuisine.

## Local Food Recommendations
Travellers visiting Srinagar should savor authentic regional delicacies, street food specialities, and seasonal sweets available at traditional market hubs.

## Travel Tips & Safety Guidelines
- Recommended Duration: 3 to 5 days.
- Best Transportation: Well connected by road, rail, and nearby airport terminals.
- Weather Preparation: Carry light woollens in winter or high-altitude evenings; carry rain gear if visiting during monsoon.
- Eco-Tourism: Keep natural trails clean and respect local heritage sites.
