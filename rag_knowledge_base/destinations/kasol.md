# Kasol, Himachal Pradesh
**Category:** Adventure  
**Best Season to Visit:** March - June  
**Average Daily Budget:** INR 2000 per person  
**Average Temperature:** 15.0 °C  

## Overview
Scenic hamlet in Parvati Valley popular among backpackers, trekkers to Kheerganga, and riverside camping.

## Key Tourist Attractions
- Sightseeing Highlights: Exploration of cultural monuments, viewpoints, and nature trails.
- Unique Landmarks: Signature heritage and scenic points celebrated across Himachal Pradesh.
- Outdoor Activities: Trekking, boating, photography, and local market strolling.

## Cultural & Historical Context
Kasol has deep regional heritage representing the cultural vibrancy of Himachal Pradesh. Local residents celebrate regional festivals with music, handloom crafts, and authentic cuisine.

## Local Food Recommendations
Travellers visiting Kasol should savor authentic regional delicacies, street food specialities, and seasonal sweets available at traditional market hubs.

## Travel Tips & Safety Guidelines
- Recommended Duration: 3 to 5 days.
- Best Transportation: Well connected by road, rail, and nearby airport terminals.
- Weather Preparation: Carry light woollens in winter or high-altitude evenings; carry rain gear if visiting during monsoon.
- Eco-Tourism: Keep natural trails clean and respect local heritage sites.
